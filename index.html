🔧 If Something Does Not Work

The preview stays black or blank

This almost always means the camera permission was not granted. Close clef-webcam, open Windows Settings, go to Privacy & security, then Camera, and make sure camera access is switched on for the app. Then start clef-webcam again.

Another program is using the camera

Windows only lets one application use a webcam at a time. Close video call apps, streaming software, and browser tabs that may be holding the camera, then restart clef-webcam.

Windows blocks the download

Right-click the downloaded file, choose Properties, and look for a checkbox at the bottom that unlocks the file. Tick it, click OK, and open the file again. You can also choose More info and then Run anyway on the prompt itself.

The download stops partway through

Interrupted downloads are usually a connection issue. Delete the partial file, return to the download page, and try again. A wired connection or a moment when the network is quiet will help.

The results feel slow

Close other demanding applications such as games, video editors, or many browser tabs. Lowering the camera resolution in Windows camera settings also helps on older machines.
